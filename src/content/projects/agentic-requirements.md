    chose: "A fast drafting and revision loop in which product owners approve every requirement before engineers receive it."
    over: "Automatically converting generated requirements into build work without product-owner review."
    because: "Requirements depended on business judgment, not extraction alone. Keeping owners accountable was also essential to adoption."
