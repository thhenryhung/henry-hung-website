decisions:
  - title: "Specialist sub-agents over one general agent"
    chose: "A router that hands each question to a sub-agent scoped to one domain."
    over: "One general-purpose agent carrying a much broader context across every product domain."
    because: "Specialists scored higher on real-question evaluations and user tests, and failures were easier to diagnose. The tradeoff was more routing logic and prompts to maintain."
  - title: "Fixing retrieval before adding capability"
    chose: "Spending the early sprints on retrieval quality against real customer questions."
    over: "Expanding into more use cases and giving the agent more actions to perform."
    because: "Interviews prioritized dependable answers, while evaluations identified retrieval as the main failure point. Together, the evidence aligned the team around fixing the foundation first."
  - title: "Escalating uncertainty instead of forcing an answer"
    chose: "Handing the conversation to a person when confidence fell below a threshold or the answer could not be grounded in an approved source."
    over: "Attempting to answer every question in order to maximize apparent automation."
    because: "A transparent handoff protected trust and made the system's limits explicit. An uncertain case was safer with a person than with a forced answer."
