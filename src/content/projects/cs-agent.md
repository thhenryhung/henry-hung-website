Customers would try once, get it, and call the contact centre anyway — which meant the chatbot was adding a step to the journey rather than removing one.\n\nSo the interesting question was not \"how do we make the bot smarter.\" It was: what are people actually asking, and what does it take to answer them? I ran more than twenty interviews with customers and with the service agents who picked up the calls the bot had given up on. The pattern that came out of those conversations is what the rest of the build was organized around."
